# M1-11 Latency probe: minute close → raw stored → cooked, per symbol

GitHub issue: #17
Lane: A      Depends on: M1-05      Owner inputs: none

## Goal
For every live minute, `pnpm live` measures how long after the minute closed it was stored in raw, and how long until its bars were cooked, cached and announced to charts, per instrument; p50 and p95 are printed and logged as a metric.

## Plan
What I found:
- In `apps/hydration/src/live.ts`, the stream's `minute` event fires once a minute is stored in raw. `LivePipeline.minute()` resolves once that minute is cooked, cached and announced to charts (or once that failed). So both moments are already visible in one place, and the probe needs no change to the pipeline's steps.
- Hydration's logs already go to CloudWatch Logs on AWS (`/aia-<env>/hydration`). A log line in CloudWatch's **embedded metric format** (EMF) becomes a metric with no extra service, agent or SDK: about $0.30 per metric a month, 4 metrics here.

Steps:
1. **`LatencyProbe`** in `contexts/hydration` (domain, pure): `record(symbol, stage, ms)` for the stages `stored` (minute close to raw stored) and `served` (minute close to cooked, cached and announced); `summary()` gives count, p50, p95 and max per symbol and stage, and clears for the next period. Vitest with hand-worked percentiles.
2. **`pnpm live`** measures each minute against its close (`bar.time + 60 s`, on the process clock):
   - every 15 minutes (`AIA_LATENCY_EVERY_MIN`), one line per instrument: `latency BTCUSD stored p50 0.8 s p95 1.4 s · served p50 0.9 s p95 1.6 s (15 minutes)`;
   - with `AIA_METRICS=emf` (set for hydration on AWS), one EMF line per minute per instrument, namespace `AIA/<env>`, metrics `MinuteStoredMs` and `MinuteServedMs`, dimension `Instrument`. CloudWatch then computes p50 and p95 itself, over any period.
   - A minute whose cooking failed is not counted as served; the failure is already logged.
3. **Infra:** `AIA_METRICS=emf` and `AIA_ENV` on the hydration task; template test.
4. **Tests:**
   - the probe;
   - the line format;
   - the EMF line shape (valid JSON, the required `_aws` block);
   - `live.ts` timing through a fake clock where it is reachable, otherwise through a small exported helper.
5. **Docs:** architecture (hydration, "Latency"), card handoff. M2-07, the freshness checker, will reuse the probe for the steps after the cache.

Not in this task:
- alarms (M4-05, needs OI-24 targets);
- the chart end of the chain (M2-07).
