## Plan
What I found:
- **The SMA lives in the private repo** and is loaded at start-up from `AIA_INDICATORS` (M3-05). `runAll` runs any loaded indicator over a list of bars and returns its drawn points at display precision: only what clients see, so the comparison stays within rule 5.
- **M3-06's `backtest`** takes bars and signals and returns trades and totals, set up as OI-30's script (initial capital 1,000,000, fixed 1 contract, no commission, no pyramiding, orders filled at the next bar's open).
- **OI-30's three exports** (`COINBASE:BTCUSD` 1h, with the "AIA SMA demo" script):
  1. chart data with the SMA plot (UNIX time);
  2. the Strategy Tester's List of trades;
  3. the Performance summary.

Steps:
1. **Readers for the exports** (`contexts/backtesting`, adapters):
   - chart data: time and OHLCV plus each plot column by name;
   - List of trades: an Entry and an Exit row per trade, with side, time, price, quantity and profit;
   - Performance summary: net profit, gross profit and loss, closed, winning and losing trades, percent profitable, profit factor, average trade.

   They find columns by their names, so a renamed or reordered column is a clear error rather than a silent misread. Times are UTC.
2. **The comparison** (pure, `contexts/backtesting`):
   - **Inputs:** TradingView's own exported bars are fed to our indicator and our backtest, as build parity uses TradingView's own bars, so a difference can only be in our logic.
   - **Plotted values:** each bar's output (for example `sma`) against the export's plot of the same name, **to the tick**: equal at the instrument's price precision. Warm-up bars must be empty on both sides.
   - **Signals:** our Long and Short markers against the trades' entries (TradingView fills at the next bar's open, so each signal is the bar before its fill), exact.
   - **Trades:** side, entry and exit time and price, quantity and profit, exact.
   - **Totals:** the Performance summary's figures against ours, exact (money to the cent, percentages to the export's 2 decimals).
   - The **last bar of each export is skipped**, as in bar parity (it was still forming when saved).
   - **The report:** per check, pass or fail, counts, and the first difference with both values and its bar.
3. **`pnpm parity:indicators [--indicator sma] [--fixtures <dir>]`** (`apps/platform/src/parity-indicators.ts`):
   - loads the indicator from `AIA_INDICATORS` as the platform does, with the default settings that OI-30's script uses (length 20, source close);
   - looks for `btcusd_1h_sma.csv`, `btcusd_1h_sma_trades.csv` and `btcusd_1h_sma_summary.csv` beside the bar exports;
   - maps the markers to signals (`long` enters long, `short` enters short);
   - exits 0 when all four checks pass, 1 when one differs, 2 when an export or the indicator is missing.

   `pnpm acceptance` picks it up on its own as check 3.
4. **Tests** on small hand-made samples, written to the export format OI-30 describes, never in `fixtures/`:
   - the readers, including a missing column;
   - each comparison passing, and failing at the right bar for a plot off by one tick, a signal one bar late, a trade with a different price, and a total off by a cent;
   - the command against a stand-in indicator from this repo's test support (not the SMA).
5. **Docs:** bar rules ("Indicator parity": file names and what is compared), architecture, card handoff.

**When OI-30 arrives:** export the three files with the names above, run `pnpm parity:indicators`, and fix any reader detail the real files differ in (header wording, date format). The comparison itself shouldn't need to change.
