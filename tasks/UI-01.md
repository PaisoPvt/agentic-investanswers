## Plan
Approved by Sham on 2026-11-19 in the project thread, with drawings saved in the browser for now (decision record T-10).
- A panel left of the chart, grouped: lines (trend, ray, extended, horizontal, horizontal ray, vertical, price line, parallel channel), Fibonacci retracement, shapes (rectangle, circle, triangle), text note; then magnet, lock all, hide all, delete all.
- KLineChart's own overlays for most tools; our own templates for the shapes and the note.
- `DrawingLayer` keeps the drawings as one overlay group; points are bar time and price, so drawings stay put across timeframes; switching instrument swaps in that instrument's drawings.
- `DrawingStore` saves them in local storage per account and instrument, guarded so the chart works where storage is blocked.
- Delete removes the selected drawing (not while locked), Esc cancels placing, a status hint says how many clicks are left.
